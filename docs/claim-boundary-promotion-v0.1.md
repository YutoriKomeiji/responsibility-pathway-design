# RPD Claim Boundary Promotion v0.1

RPD treats public claims as evidence-governed states. A current research non-claim is not automatically a permanent disclaimer.

RPD distinguishes:

1. **evidence-limited research boundaries** that can move when declared review, empirical, comparative, and operational evidence is obtained; and
2. **permanent responsibility boundaries** that a design-translation framework should not cross by itself.

## Current evidence boundary

RPD v0.1 is a **provisional, reviewable research baseline**. This supports claims about internal coherence, inspectability, documented transformation steps, explicit assurance/operational-governance interfaces, and the declared D/I/X/O/V verification vocabulary.

It does not yet establish broad external validity, comparative superiority, general empirical effectiveness, standardization, certification, or production/operational authorization.

## Promotion criteria

| Current boundary | Evidence that can move it |
|---|---|
| Internal coherence only | independent structured review, issue/counterexample resolution, cross-document traceability and reproducible worked-case review |
| Limited empirical validation | predeclared empirical protocols, coded case corpus, adjudication, study results, negative/counterexample cases and replication where feasible |
| No comparative-effectiveness claim | explicit baselines/alternative design methods, comparison criteria, matched cases or controlled evaluations, and transparent failure analysis |
| Limited operational validation | exercise and operational evidence using the D/I/X/O/V vocabulary, including intervention timing, returnability, contestability, recovery and residual stewardship evidence |
| No broad contextual validity claim | multi-domain/multi-context studies, affected-party and institutional review, boundary-condition analysis and theory revision where findings fail |
| No standardization/certification claim | independent governance/standards processes external to the repository; RPD artifacts alone cannot self-promote into an official standard or certification scheme |

Promotion is explicit. A successful worked example, design audit, or downstream implementation does not by itself establish broad empirical validity.

## Permanent responsibility boundaries

- RPD does not create, automatically interpret, or finally determine law, policy, ethics, standards, or affected-party mandates.
- RPD does not determine legal liability or transfer final responsibility to AI.
- RPD does not replace systems safety, human factors, requirements engineering, assurance, incident response, or institutional governance.
- RPD does not turn documentation, logging, or technical rollback into completed responsibility recovery by itself.
- RPD does not make an assurance record self-authorizing certification.
- Final operational authorization, continuation, suspension, redesign, retirement, remedy, and residual-impact stewardship remain with responsible humans/institutions.

These are responsibility boundaries, not unfinished research milestones.

## Evidence owners and promotion states

RPD research/design work owns definitions, transformation logic, pattern/evaluation records, falsification criteria and declared design-level evidence. RPE/runtime/operation may provide implementation, exercise and operational evidence but cannot automatically promote RPD claims without explicit review. External researchers, affected parties, practitioners and institutions are needed for broader validation. Legal, certification and operational authority remains external.

Where practical, evidence-limited boundaries use `evidence_collecting`, `review_ready`, or `promoted`; permanent boundaries use `permanently_out_of_scope`.
